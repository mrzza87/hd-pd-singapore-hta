# Data sources

This file summarises the source inventory used to generate the raw and processed model inputs. Full source-to-parameter mapping is available in `data_processed/source_inventory.csv` and `data_processed/input_quality_flags.csv`.

## COOPER_2020_UTILITIES

- **Title:** Cooper JT et al. Health related quality of life utility weights for economic evaluation through different stages of chronic kidney disease: a systematic review. 2020.

- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7507735/

- **Data origin:** Systematic review of CKD health-state utility evidence

## DRUMMOND_2015

- **Title:** Drummond MF et al. Methods for the Economic Evaluation of Health Care Programmes. 4th ed. Oxford University Press; 2015.

- **Data origin:** Health economic evaluation textbook

## DUKE_MYKIDNEY_COSTS

- **Title:** Duke-NUS Lien Centre for Palliative Care. myKIDNEY: Comparing treatment options

- **URL:** https://www.duke-nus.edu.sg/lcpc/mykidney/treatment-options/comparing-treatment-options

- **Data origin:** Singapore patient-facing HD and PD cost ranges

## HEALTHHUB_PD_COSTS

- **Title:** HealthHub Singapore. Peritoneal Dialysis

- **URL:** https://www.healthhub.sg/health-conditions/what-is-peritoneal-dialysis

- **Data origin:** Singapore patient-facing PD cost information

## KHAN_2022_ECONOMICS

- **Title:** Khan BA et al. Health economics of kidney replacement therapy in Singapore: Taking stock and looking ahead. Ann Acad Med Singap. 2022.

- **URL:** https://annals.edu.sg/health-economics-of-kidney-replacement-therapy-in-singapore-taking-stock-and-looking-ahead/

- **Data origin:** Singapore health economics discussion of kidney replacement therapy

## KHOO_2022_OUTCOMES

- **Title:** Khoo CY et al. Death and cardiovascular outcomes in end-stage renal failure patients on different modalities of dialysis. Ann Acad Med Singap. 2022.

- **URL:** https://annals.edu.sg/death-and-cardiovascular-outcomes-in-end-stage-renal-failure-patients-on-different-modalities-of-dialysis/

- **Data origin:** Singapore population-based dialysis modality outcome study

## MOH_PD_POLICY

- **Title:** Ministry of Health Singapore. Key causes of recent increase in number of patients requiring kidney dialysis

- **URL:** https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/key-causes-of-recent-increase-in-number-of-patients-requiring-kidney-dialysis/

- **Data origin:** Singapore policy statement on PD-preferred strategy and PD uptake target

## NKF_KEY_STATS

- **Title:** National Kidney Foundation Singapore. Key Statistics

- **URL:** https://nkfs.org/about-us/key-statistics/

- **Data origin:** Singapore kidney failure and dialysis burden statistics

## SG_MTE_METHODS

- **Title:** Singapore Medical Technology Evaluation Methods and Process Guide

- **URL:** https://www.ace-hta.gov.sg/resources/process-methods/

- **Data origin:** Singapore medical technology evaluation methods source

## YANG_2016_CEA

- **Title:** Yang F, Lau T, Luo N. Cost-effectiveness of haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis for patients with end-stage renal disease in Singapore. Nephrology. 2016.

- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26566750/

- **Data origin:** Singapore peer-reviewed cost-effectiveness model

## YANG_2018_PD_HRQoL

- **Title:** Yang F et al. Health-Related Quality of Life in Patients Treated with Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis and Automated Peritoneal Dialysis in Singapore.

- **URL:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41669-017-0046-z

- **Data origin:** Singapore PD health-related quality-of-life evidence
